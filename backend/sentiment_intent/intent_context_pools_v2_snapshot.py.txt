"""Intent pools — intent carried by situation, not by a stock intent phrase.

Separate from :mod:`phrase_pools` (the v3 pools the NER corpus and the old intent
head were built on) and from :mod:`pilot_emotion_pools` (sentiment). Nothing
here feeds either of those corpora.

Why a new pool set exists
-------------------------
The intent head trained on v6 reached 1.000 test macro-F1 and a phrase matcher
over the generator's own pools reached the same 1.000. On the unseen-50 set it
scored 57% where the caller stated an intent and 2.6% where the intent had to
be inferred: "We want to buy a 2 BHK" came back Buy at 0.98, the same sentence
with "want to buy" removed came back Inquiry at 0.32. 1456 of the 1504 v6
transcripts carry a stock phrase for their own intent, so the model learned
those phrases and nothing else — the same failure v3 sentiment had with affect
words, fixed the same way:

1. **No stock intent vocabulary.** No phrase here says buy, purchase, rent,
   invest, visit, call back, enquiry or information. The intent is in the
   situation — a sanctioned loan, a deposit question, "nobody in my family
   will live there". :func:`stated_intent_leaks` enforces the ban.

2. **A group is an idea, not a phrasing.** ``{intent: {idea: [surface forms]}}``,
   three forms per idea. The phrase-holdout diagnostic withholds whole ideas,
   so a held-out transcript expresses an intent through a situation the model
   has never seen at all.

3. **Cue words are shared across intents on purpose.** A monthly figure is not
   Rent: it is also an EMI (Buy) and a tenant's payment (Investment). "Loan"
   is Buy, but also Inquiry (which banks are tied up) and Investment (the EMI
   the tenant covers). "Saturday" is Visit and Callback; "phone" is Callback
   and Inquiry; "owner" is Rent and Buy. Each intent also carries *contrast*
   ideas that mention a rival intent's vocabulary to rule it out ("owning is
   too big a step for us right now" is Rent). :func:`lopsided_tokens` reports
   any content word that stays exclusive to one intent across several ideas.

4. **Written after the test set, around it.** ``backend/eval/
   intent_unseen_cases.py`` was fixed first. These ideas cover the same six
   intents through different situations, and ``eval.run_intent_unseen``
   measures the overlap rather than assuming it away.

Sentiment pools are re-exported so ``sentiment_intent.data._phrase_pools``
keeps working when handed this module; they are not used to label anything.
"""

INTENT_PHRASE_GROUPS: Dict[str, Dict[str, List[str]]] = INTENT_IDEAS

    label: [form for forms in ideas.values() for form in forms]
    for label, ideas in INTENT_IDEAS.items()
}


def phrase_register(text: str) -> str:
    """'code-mixed' or 'english' — for an idea key (``cm-`` prefix) or a phrase."""
    if " " not in text.strip():
        return "code-mixed" if text.startswith("cm-") else "english"
    tokens = set(re.findall(r"[a-z]+", text.lower()))
    return "code-mixed" if tokens & set(_HINDI_MARKERS) else "english"


def stated_intent_leaks() -> List[Tuple[str, str, str]]:
    """(intent, idea, phrase) for every phrase that names an intent outright."""
    leaks = []
    for label, ideas in INTENT_IDEAS.items():
        for idea, forms in ideas.items():
            for form in forms:
                if any(re.search(p, form.lower()) for p in STOCK_INTENT_PATTERNS):
                    leaks.append((label, idea, form))
    return leaks


_STOP = frozenset(
    "a an the and or but so if of to in on at for with from by is are was be it its "
    "i we you me my our us your this that these those there here what which who when "
    "how can could would will i'm we're it's don't i'd i'll we'd we'll just about "
    "not no yes one two three all any some more than then now up out as do does".split()
)


def lopsided_tokens(min_ideas: int = 3) -> Dict[str, Tuple[str, int]]:
    """Content words that appear in ``min_ideas``+ ideas of one intent and no other.

    Those are the candidate shortcuts: a model can score them without reading
    the situation. Some are legitimate (a monthly advance really is Rent), so
    this is reported, not enforced.
    """
    ideas_with: Dict[str, collections.Counter] = collections.defaultdict(collections.Counter)
    for label, ideas in INTENT_IDEAS.items():
        for forms in ideas.values():
            tokens = set(re.findall(r"[a-z']+", " ".join(forms).lower())) - _STOP
            for token in tokens:
                ideas_with[token][label] += 1
    return {
        token: next(iter(counts.items()))
        for token, counts in ideas_with.items()
        if len(counts) == 1 and next(iter(counts.values())) >= min_ideas
    }
